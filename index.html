What we do

Advice for every stage of the journey

Three areas of work, mapped to where you are: planning the switch, getting the infrastructure right, and optimising a fleet that is already electric.

🗺️

Electrification planning

Which vehicles to switch and when, total cost of ownership against fuel, and what the transition looks like operationally. We give you a clear, evidence-based view before you commit any budget. For a quick first estimate of the numbers, try our free TCO Calculator.

🔌

Infrastructure and charging strategy

Charger numbers and power ratings, site capacity and grid connection, tariff selection, and a charging strategy around your duty cycles. Getting this right early avoids expensive demand charges. We specify requirements, sanity-check supplier quotes, and bring in trusted partners for specialist work like electrical design and grid flexibility participation.

📊

Charging analysis and optimisation

For fleets already running EVs. We analyse your charging data and tariffs to surface waste and patterns you cannot see from the invoice, then recommend practical operational changes that cut costs. You get clear priorities to act on straight away, with no new software required.

How it works

A simple process that adds value at every step

Each stage is designed to give you something useful, starting with the very first conversation. We meet you where you are and build from there.

01

Free intro call

A friendly conversation about your fleet, where you are now, and what you want to achieve. It is free, and you will come away with useful pointers either way.

02

Share your data

Charging records, tariffs, and fleet schedules. Whatever you have to hand is enough to get started.

03

Analysis

We run the numbers with AI-assisted, data-driven analysis, testing your data against the options that matter for your operation.

04

Clear report and recommendations

A concise report you can act on, written for the people who make the decisions. Practical steps and priorities, not a research paper, with optional follow-up support if you would like a hand putting them into practice.

About

Meet the founder

Gridicity is run by me, Alicia Blatiak. I founded it to give fleets independent, data-driven advice on going electric, free of any vendor agenda.

I hold a PhD in Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) technology from Imperial College London and an MPhil from the University of Cambridge, and I have worked on transportation and energy systems at DNV GL and the International Renewable Energy Agency. That research background sits behind every recommendation: rigorous analysis, clear evidence, and practical answers you can act on.
