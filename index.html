The data advantage

Your charging data is your most underused asset

Most organisations collect charging session data and do very little with it. Every session contains signals about utilisation efficiency, energy cost, charger health, and driver behaviour. Aggregated over time, that data tells a precise story about where your fleet stands and where the biggest gains are.

  • Which chargers are being used at peak-rate periods and what that is costing annually
  • Which vehicles are consistently undercharging or missing sessions entirely
  • Where demand peaks are occurring and whether they are triggering capacity charges
  • How actual energy consumption compares to manufacturer range estimates across your specific routes
  • Where smart scheduling would have the greatest financial impact

Our analysis ingests your session exports or API feed and returns a structured findings report with prioritised recommendations. AI agents handle the data processing automatically, so the turnaround is fast and the findings are specific to your operation.
