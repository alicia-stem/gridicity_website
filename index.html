Core capabilities

Smarter charging, automatically

Gridicity continuously analyses energy markets, grid conditions, and your fleet's schedule to find the cheapest, most efficient charging windows.

⚡

Variable Tariff Optimisation

Energy prices fluctuate throughout the day, week, and season. Gridicity monitors supply, demand, and real-time pricing to automatically schedule charging during the lowest-cost windows — without compromising fleet availability.

⚖️

Dynamic Load Management

Distribute a limited energy supply intelligently across multiple EV chargers on a single site. Gridicity prevents costly capacity overages and demand-charge penalties by balancing load in real time.

🔋

Grid Capacity Services

When the grid is strained, Gridicity can pause charging and sell that demand flexibility back to the grid — generating revenue while helping balance the network. Your fleet always maintains minimum required charge.
