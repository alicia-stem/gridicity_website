What we do

End-to-end fleet electrification support

We bring together technical, commercial, and operational expertise to help organisations navigate every stage of the EV transition.

📋

Fleet Assessment & Strategy

We analyse your existing fleet across routes, duty cycles, utilisation patterns, and fuel spend to identify which vehicles are ready to electrify now and what the full transition roadmap looks like. Our AI modelling runs thousands of scenarios against your real operational data so you get answers grounded in fact, not assumption.

Fleet audit TCO modelling Transition roadmap
⚡

Charging Infrastructure Planning

Site surveys, load capacity assessments, charger specification, and procurement support. We help you choose the right charging infrastructure for your operation and avoid costly over- or under-investment.

Site surveys Charger specification Grid connection advice
💷

Business Case & Funding

We build the financial case for electrification, modelling total cost of ownership, identifying available grants and incentives, and preparing the documentation needed to secure board or funder approval. Our automated TCO models update in real time as vehicle prices, energy tariffs, and grant programmes change, so your business case stays current.

Grant identification TCO analysis Board-ready reports
🔌

Tariff & Energy Strategy

Charging cost is the largest ongoing variable in EV fleet economics. We advise on tariff selection, smart charging strategy, and energy procurement to minimise your electricity bill. We also connect clients to Gridicity's AI scheduling tools, which automate off-peak charging across your whole site and take the ongoing complexity off your plate.

Tariff optimisation Energy procurement Smart charging strategy
🏢

Supplier & Procurement Support

From vehicle OEM selection to charge point operator procurement, we provide independent advice and support tender processes so you get the right solution at the right price without vendor lock-in risk. We use market pricing data and AI-assisted comparisons to cut through supplier noise and identify genuine value.

Vehicle selection CPO tendering Independent advice

Operational Change Management

Technology is only half the challenge. We support the people side of fleet electrification: driver communications, training, policy updates, and embedding new processes so the transition sticks. Automated reporting gives fleet managers a live view of progress without adding to their workload.

How we work

A structured path to electrification

Every engagement follows a clear, repeatable process, adapted to your organisation's size, timeline, and starting point. AI-assisted tools run in the background at each stage, so we spend more time on insight and less on number crunching.

01

Discovery

We start with a structured conversation about your fleet, your constraints, and your goals. No obligation, just clarity on what electrification would mean for you and a rough sense of the numbers before we go any further.

02

Fleet audit & analysis

We review your telematics data, fuel records, and operational requirements to build a detailed picture of electrification readiness across your whole fleet.

03

Strategy & business case

We deliver a clear recommendation: which vehicles to electrify, in what order, and with what infrastructure. Our AI modelling generates the financial case automatically, so you get a board-ready report without weeks of spreadsheet work.

04

Delivery support

We stay with you through procurement, installation, and go-live, providing independent oversight to keep the project on track. Automated progress dashboards give your team visibility at every stage without needing to chase updates from suppliers.
